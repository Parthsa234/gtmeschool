Things people ask before enrolling

Q.01Do I need to know how to code?+

No. The machines are built in Clay, Claude and a sequencer. Where a script helps, we give you the script and show you what it does.

Q.02Is it live or recorded?+

The builds and the 1:1s are live, with your work on screen. Recordings stay in the vault so you can rebuild anything later.

Q.03What will the tools cost me?+

Week one gives you a cost sheet for your own sending volume before you buy anything, so you don't overpay for tools. Many students start on what their company already pays for.

Q.04How many hours a week?+

8–10 hours a week for eight weeks, in both programmes.

Q.05Can I pay in instalments?+

In India, yes: Lieutenant can be paid over 12–24 months through our financing partner, with the fee shown before you commit. Companies get a GST invoice and can pay by PO. The International Programme is invoiced in USD.

Q.06Which programme is for me?+

If you're in India and paying in INR, Lieutenant: ₹3.8L including GST, with weekly 1:1 time with Nish. If you're outside India, the International Programme: $6,000, in your time zone. The call tells you straight if it isn't a fit.

Q.07Will this get me a job?+

We don't know, and nobody honest does. What we can show you is what people built and what those machines produced. You leave with real campaigns and real numbers to show a hiring manager or a client, which is more than a certificate.

Q.08I'm outside India. Does this work for me?+

Yes. That's what the International Programme is for: $6,000 in USD, built for the English-speaking market, with two time-zone slots. Book a call first.

Q.09What's the refund policy?+

Full refund before week three if the programme isn't what we described, EMI included.
